## 📚 Publications

### ✍️ Author / Co-author

1. **Med-LLaMA3: Advancing Medical Question-Answering Through Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning of Large Language Models** — LoRA/QLoRA 4-bit fine-tuning of LLaMA-3 on a curated **1.5M+ sample** medical instruction dataset; **75.71 % avg. accuracy** on medical MMLU subsets while training only ~4 % of parameters and cutting memory ~75 %.
2. **SLM with Swarm Intelligence for Efficient Representation of Medical Claims** — biomedical embeddings (BioBERT, BioMegatron, GatorTron) + swarm-intelligence feature selection (PSO, GWO, GA, HHO, SHADE) for claim fraud detection; **~50 % dimensionality reduction, 87 % accuracy, 96 % specificity**.
3. **DistilLLM-Med: A Lightweight Medical Language Model through Knowledge Distillation** — distilling Med42-8B & MedGemma-4B into LLaMA-3.2-1B with temperature-scaled KL, specialty-weighted loss and attention alignment; **75 % smaller, up to 67.8 % teacher retention, +20 % over base**.
4. **Fraud Detection and Explanation in Medical Claims Using GNN Architectures** — graph neural networks for detecting and explaining fraudulent claims on real-world MENA data.
5. **A Survey on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Models for Healthcare Applications** — taxonomy of Naive / Advanced / Modular RAG across text, visual and hybrid medical domains.
6. **Generative AI with Big Data for Better Detection of Fraud in Medical Claims** — LLM text-to-embedding fraud detection on MENA data; Gemini outperformed BERT-based models at **90.44 % accuracy**.
7. **GNN as Explainable Tool with Heterogeneous and Homogeneous Data for Medical Claim Validation** — GNNExplainer & PGExplainer evaluated on heterogeneous / homogeneous GNNs.
8. **Fraud Detection in Medical Insurance Claims Using Majority Voting of Multiple Unsupervised Algorithms** — novel majority-voting ensemble validated by expert review on Gulf-region datasets.
